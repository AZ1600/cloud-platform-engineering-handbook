# Phase 8 — Technical Interviews & Engineering Evidence

> A practice workbook built from source inspected on 11 October 2026. Repository code, test definitions and documentation are evidence of different kinds; none automatically proves continuous production performance or personal employment experience.

This final deep-dive phase turns the handbook's seven earlier tracks into **interview conversations supported by verifiable work**. It is not a collection of memorised answers. Your aim is to explain a decision, describe a request or failure path, identify the evidence, recognise limits and propose the next test.

## How to practise

1. Choose a target role and identify two required skills.
2. Answer aloud **without opening the suggested answer** where one is provided.
3. Draw a minimal component and trust-boundary diagram for each system-design prompt.
4. Cite a source file, a test and a limitation; do not claim you ran it unless you did.
5. Score the answer with the [mock interview rubric](11-mock-interview-loop.md).
6. Record an improvement and repeat the same question a week later.

**Evidence categories:** implemented source, test defined, test observed passing, documented demonstration, cloud-validated at a particular time, proposed design. Distinguish these in every answer.
